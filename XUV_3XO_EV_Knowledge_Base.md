Product Name: Mahindra XUV 3XO EV
Tagline: "EVerything you want. Now electric."
Category: Electric Compact SUV

Variants Available: AX5, AX7L

## POWERTRAIN & PERFORMANCE
- Battery Capacity: 39.4 kWh
- Motor Power: 110 kW (150 PS)
- Motor Torque: 310 Nm
- Drive Type: FWD (Front Wheel Drive)
- Acceleration: 0–100 km/h in 8.3 seconds
- Drive Modes: Fun, Fast, Fearless (Multi Drive Modes)
- Single Pedal Drive: Yes
- Range (MIDC P1 + P2): 351 km
- Range (MIDC P1): 456 km
- Real World Range: Up to 285 km (with AC on; subject to change based on driving conditions)

## CHARGING
- Charger Type / Standard: CCS Type II
- AC Portable Charger: 13A (up to 3.0 kW), compatible with household 3-pin 16A socket
- AC Charging Time (0–100% SOC) with 7.2 kW charger: 6.5 hours
- DC Fast Charging Time (0–80% SOC) with 50 kW DC charger: 50 minutes
- Wall Mounted Unit (AX5 variant): 3.3 kW / 16A socket
- Fast Charging (AX7L variant): 65W USB-C Fast Charging (for devices)

## QUICK FAQ FOR CALLING AGENT

Q: What is the range of the XUV 3XO EV?
A: MIDC P1+P2 range is 351 km, and MIDC P1 range is 456 km. Real-world range with AC on is up to approximately 285 km, depending on driving conditions.

Q: How fast can it charge?
A: With a 50 kW DC fast charger, it charges from 0–80% in 50 minutes. With a 7.2 kW AC charger, a full 0–100% charge takes about 6.5 hours. It also supports a portable 13A/3kW AC charger on a standard household socket.

Q: What is the battery and motor specification?
A: 39.4 kWh battery, 110 kW (150 PS) motor power, 310 Nm torque, front-wheel drive, 0–100 km/h in 8.3 seconds.

Q: What variants are available?
A: Two variants — AX5 (base) and AX7L (top-spec with ADAS, panoramic skyroof, Harman Kardon audio, 360-degree camera, and more).

Q: What colours are available?
A: Everest White, Nebula Blue, Stealth Black, Deep Forest, Tango Red, and Galaxy Grey.

Q: What is the warranty?
A: 8 years or 160,000 km for the battery pack and motor; 3 years or unlimited km for the vehicle (whichever comes first).

Q: Does it have safety features like ADAS?
A: The AX7L variant includes Level 2 ADAS along with 6 airbags, ESP, and a 360-degree surround view camera. Both variants include 6 airbags, ESP, and TPMS as standard.

Q: What is the seating capacity?
A: 5-seater.
