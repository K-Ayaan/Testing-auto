## 5. ENGINES & TRANSMISSIONS

### Petrol — mStallion (Turbo Petrol Direct Injection / TGDI)
- Capacity: 2.0 L
- Turbocharger: eWGT (Electric Wastegate Turbocharger, Intercooled)
- Drivetrain: RWD only
- 6-Speed Manual Transmission:
  - Max Power: 119 kW @ 5000 rpm
  - Max Torque: 330 Nm @ 1500–3000 rpm
- 6-Speed Torque Converter Automatic:
  - Max Power: 130 kW @ 5000 rpm
  - Max Torque: 380 Nm @ 1750–3000 rpm

### Diesel — mHawk (Common Rail Direct Injection)
- Capacity: 2.2 L
- Turbocharger: eVGT (Electric Variable Geometry Turbocharger, Intercooled)
- Available in RWD and 4x4
- 6-Speed Manual Transmission:
  - Max Power: 111.9 kW @ 3750 rpm
  - Max Torque: 330 Nm @ 1500–3000 rpm
- 6-Speed Torque Converter Automatic (RWD):
  - Max Power: 111.9 kW @ 3750 rpm
  - Max Torque: 330 Nm @ 1500–3000 rpm
- 6-Speed Torque Converter Automatic (4x4 — higher output tune):
  - Max Power: 128.6 kW @ 3500 rpm
  - Max Torque: 400 Nm @ 1750–2750 rpm

### 4x4 System
- Manual Shift Part-Time 4x4 with High & Low Reduction Gear
- ELD (Electronic Locking Differential)
- 2H mode with front axle disconnect
