MAHINDRA THAR ROXX KNOWLEDGE BASE
Source Official Thar Roxx Brochure For use in AI calling agent support knowledge base

1 PRODUCT OVERVIEW
- Vehicle Name: Mahindra Thar Roxx
- Marketing Tagline: THE SUV
- Category: Sport Utility Vehicle (SUV), sub-4.5m segment
- Positioning: 5-door, 5-seater evolution of the Mahindra Thar, built on the all-new M_GLYDE platform
- Key theme: Bold design, off-road capability, premium interior, advanced safety and tech

2 DESIGN AND EXTERIOR HIGHLIGHTS
- Short overhangs, wider stance, upright A-pillar, flat windshield for a commanding street presence
- External door hinges, handlebars, tailgate-mounted spare wheel
- Longer wheelbase than the previous generation
- Flat, solid, smooth exterior surfaces
- Dual-Tone Metal Roof: solid metal roof with sleek black matte finish (adventure aesthetic and durability)
- Note: Dual-tone roofs on Stealth Black vehicles available only on AX7L and AX5L variants
- MX1, MX3, AX3L, MX5 have body-coloured roofs when finished in Stealth Black
- C-shaped LED Daytime Running Lamps (DRLs) at the front; matching iconic LED taillamps at rear
- New front grille design bold, polished trim
- R19 Diamond Cut Alloy Wheels (AX7L) set within trapezoidal wheel arches; class-leading All-Terrain tyres

3 DIMENSIONS AND CAPACITIES
- Length: 4428 mm
- Width: 1870 mm
- Height: 1923 mm
- Wheelbase: 2850 mm (highest in segment)
- Track Width (Front and Rear): 1580 mm
- Approach Angle: 41.7 degrees
- Departure Angle: 36.1 degrees
- Ramp-over Angle: 23.9 degrees
- Water Wading Depth: 650 mm
- Seating Capacity: 5
- Fuel Tank Capacity: 57 L

4 PLATFORM AND CHASSIS
- All-New M_GLYDE PLATFORM: high-strength steel chassis
- Benefits: high torsional stiffness, lighter weight, highest wheelbase in segment
- Delivers improved stability, comfort, and dynamics handling balance

5 ENGINES AND TRANSMISSIONS
- Petrol mStallion (Turbo Petrol Direct Injection / TGDI)
- Capacity: 2.0 L
- Turbocharger: eWGT (Electric Wastegate Turbocharger, Intercooled)
- Drivetrain: RWD only
- 6-Speed Manual Transmission Max Power: 119 kW at 5000 rpm
- 6-Speed Manual Transmission Max Torque: 330 Nm at 1500 to 3000 rpm
- 6-Speed Torque Converter Automatic Max Power: 130 kW at 5000 rpm
- 6-Speed Torque Converter Automatic Max Torque: 380 Nm at 1750 to 3000 rpm

- Diesel mHawk (Common Rail Direct Injection)
- Capacity: 2.2 L
- Turbocharger: eVGT (Electric Variable Geometry Turbocharger, Intercooled)
- Available in RWD and 4x4
- 6-Speed Manual Transmission Max Power: 111.9 kW at 3750 rpm
- 6-Speed Manual Transmission Max Torque: 330 Nm at 1500 to 3000 rpm
- 6-Speed Torque Converter Automatic (RWD) Max Power: 111.9 kW at 3750 rpm
- 6-Speed Torque Converter Automatic (RWD) Max Torque: 330 Nm at 1500 to 3000 rpm
- 6-Speed Torque Converter Automatic (4x4 higher output tune) Max Power: 128.6 kW at 3500 rpm
- 6-Speed Torque Converter Automatic (4x4 higher output tune) Max Torque: 400 Nm at 1750 to 2750 rpm

- 4x4 System
- Manual Shift Part-Time 4x4 with High and Low Reduction Gear
- ELD (Electronic Locking Differential)
- 2H mode with front axle disconnect

- Contains 35 plus standard safety features overall
- Up to 6 Airbags including Driver, Passenger, Side, and Curtain
- Comprehensive ESP 9.3 suite featuring Electronic Stability Control, EBD, ABS, Corner Braking Control, Engine Drag Torque Control, Vehicle Dynamics Control, Hill Hold Control, Hill Descent Control, Hydraulic Brake Assist, Traction Control, Roll Over Mitigation, Emergency Stop Signal, Brake Locking Differential, Brake Disc Wiping
- Automatic Emergency Braking with Vehicles, Pedestrian, and Cyclist detection (variant dependent)
- Tyre Pressure Monitoring System
- Parking Sensors and Rear Camera
- 3-Point Seat Belts for all seats and Seat Belt Reminder with Occupant Detection System
- Front Seat Belt Pretensioners and Height-Adjustable Front Seat Belts
- Day and Night Auto-Dimming IRVM
- Impact Sensing Auto Door Unlock
- Immobilizer, E-Call, and SOS
- ISOFIX and i-Size child seat compatibility
- Rear Disc Brakes on select variants
- Electric Park Brake with Auto Hold on Tech Pack L variants
