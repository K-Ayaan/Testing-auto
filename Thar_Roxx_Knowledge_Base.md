## 5. ENGINES & TRANSMISSIONS

### Petrol — mStallion (Turbo Petrol Direct Injection / TGDI)
- Capacity: 2.0 L
- Turbocharger: eWGT (Electric Wastegate Turbocharger, Intercooled)
- Drivetrain: RWD only
- 6-Speed Manual Transmission:
  - Max Power: 119 kW @ 5000 rpm
  - Max Torque: 330 Nm @ 1500–3000 rpm
- 6-Speed Torque Converter Automatic:
  - Max Power: 130 kW @ 5000 rpm
  - Max Torque: 380 Nm @ 1750–3000 rpm

### Diesel — mHawk (Common Rail Direct Injection)
- Capacity: 2.2 L
- Turbocharger: eVGT (Electric Variable Geometry Turbocharger, Intercooled)
- Available in RWD and 4x4
- 6-Speed Manual Transmission:
  - Max Power: 111.9 kW @ 3750 rpm
  - Max Torque: 330 Nm @ 1500–3000 rpm
- 6-Speed Torque Converter Automatic (RWD):
  - Max Power: 111.9 kW @ 3750 rpm
  - Max Torque: 330 Nm @ 1500–3000 rpm
- 6-Speed Torque Converter Automatic (4x4 — higher output tune):
  - Max Power: 128.6 kW @ 3500 rpm
  - Max Torque: 400 Nm @ 1750–2750 rpm

### 4x4 System
- Manual Shift Part-Time 4x4 with High & Low Reduction Gear
- ELD (Electronic Locking Differential)
- 2H mode with front axle disconnect

## 7. SAFETY

- Bharat NCAP Rating: **5 Stars** — "'THE SUV' with 5 Stars Safety Rating"
  - Adult Occupant Rating: 31.09
  - Child Occupant Rating: 45.00
- 35+ standard safety features overall, including:
  - Up to 6 Airbags (Driver, Passenger, Side, Curtain)
  - Comprehensive ESP 9.3 suite (Electronic Stability Control, EBD, ABS, Corner Braking Control, Engine Drag Torque Control, Vehicle Dynamics Control, Hill Hold Control, Hill Descent Control, Hydraulic Brake Assist, Traction Control, Roll Over Mitigation, Emergency Stop Signal, Brake Locking Differential, Brake Disc Wiping)
  - Automatic Emergency Braking (Vehicles, Pedestrian, Cyclist detection — variant dependent)
  - Tyre Pressure Monitoring System
  - Parking Sensors & Rear Camera
  - 3-Point Seat Belts for all seats + Seat Belt Reminder (all seats) with Occupant Detection System
  - Front Seat Belt Pretensioners, Height-Adjustable Front Seat Belts
  - Day/Night Auto-Dimming IRVM
  - Impact Sensing Auto Door Unlock
  - Immobilizer
  - E-Call & SOS
  - ISOFIX / i-Size child seat compatibility
  - Rear Disc Brakes (select variants)
  - Electric Park Brake with Auto Hold (Tech Pack "L" variants)

### ADAS Level 2 Suite
- 10 features, specifically trained/tuned for Indian road conditions
- Includes: Forward Collision Warning (FCW), Autonomous Emergency Braking (vehicle/pedestrian/cyclist), Adaptive Cruise Control with Stop & Go, Lane Departure Warning (LDW), Lane Keep Assist (LKA), Smart Pilot Assist (SPA), High Beam Assist (HBA), Traffic Sign Recognition (TSR)
- Note: Smart Pilot Assist and Adaptive Cruise Control available only on Tech Pack (L) Automatic variants
- Disclaimer: ADAS assists the driver but is not a substitute for driver vigilance; driver remains fully responsible for safe driving

## 8. SUSPENSION & RIDE

- Front: Independent Front Suspension — Double Wishbone with Coil Spring, FDD (Frequency Dependent Damping) & MTV-CL (Multi Tuning Valve – Concentric Land) dampers
- Rear: Rigid Axle Coil Spring with Watt's Linkage, FDD, HRS (Hydraulic Rebound Stopper), MTV-CL dampers
- AHRS (Advanced Hydraulic Rebound Stop) — Thar Roxx claimed to be first SUV to offer this, improving body control on undulated roads
- Brakes: Ventilated Disc (Front) & Rear Disc/Rear Drum (variant dependent)
- Steering: Electric Power Steering with Tilt functionality

---

## 9. OFF-ROAD / DRIVE TECHNOLOGY

- **CrawlSmart**: Detects obstacles and automatically adjusts throttle response for optimal power on climbs — claimed first among Indian OEMs
- **IntelliTurn**: Assists in navigating tight U-turns/off-road trails by optimizing turning response
- **Electronic Locking Differential (ELD)**: Locks rear axle within microseconds for max traction — claimed first among Indian OEMs
- **4XPLOR Terrain Modes**: Snow, Mud, Sand (combined with ELD)
- **XPLOR Terrain Modes**: Snow, Sand, Mud (lower trims, single system)
- **Drive Modes**: Zip and Zoom (for on-road/highway driving)
- Electric driveline disconnect on front axle
- Water Wading Depth: 650 mm
- Washable interior mats with drain plug

## 18. SUGGESTED Q&A PAIRS FOR AI AGENT

**Q: What engines does the Thar Roxx offer?**
A: Two engines — a 2.0L mStallion turbo-petrol (RWD only, manual or automatic) and a 2.2L mHawk diesel (available in both RWD and 4x4, manual or automatic).

**Q: What is the safety rating of the Thar Roxx?**
A: It has received a 5-Star Bharat NCAP safety rating, with an Adult Occupant Rating of 31.09 and Child Occupant Rating of 45.00.

**Q: Which variants get 4x4?**
A: 4x4 (diesel, manual or automatic) is available on the MX5 and AX7L variants.

**Q: Does the Thar Roxx have a sunroof?**
A: Yes, it comes with SkyRoof™, described as the largest panoramic sunroof in the sub-4.5m SUV segment, available on select variants.

**Q: What is the wheelbase and why does it matter?**
A: 2850 mm — the highest in its segment, contributing to greater stability, ride comfort, and interior space.

**Q: What ADAS features are included?**
A: A Level 2 ADAS suite with 10 features including Forward Collision Warning, Autonomous Emergency Braking, Adaptive Cruise Control with Stop & Go, Lane Departure Warning, Lane Keep Assist, Smart Pilot Assist, High Beam Assist, and Traffic Sign Recognition (feature availability depends on variant).

**Q: What off-road technologies does it have?**
A: CrawlSmart (automatic throttle modulation for climbs), IntelliTurn (assisted tight turning), Electronic Locking Differential (ELD), and 4XPLOR terrain modes (Snow, Mud, Sand), plus 650 mm water wading depth.

**Q: What colours are available?**
A: Stealth Black, Tango Red, Everest White, Deep Forest, Nebula Blue, Battleship Grey, and Burnt Sienna.

**Q: What is the customer support number?**
A: 1800 209 6006 (toll-free, "With You Hamesha").
