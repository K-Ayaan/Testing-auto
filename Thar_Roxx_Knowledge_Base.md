PRODUCT OVERVIEW
Mahindra Thar Roxx is a 5 door and 5 seater SUV built on the M_GLYDE platform with the marketing tagline THE SUV. It is positioned in the sub 4.5m segment with a design theme focused on bold aesthetics, off road capability, premium interior, and advanced tech.

DESIGN AND EXTERIOR HIGHLIGHTS
The vehicle feature short overhangs, wider stance, upright A-pillar, and a flat windshield for a commanding street presence. It includes external door hinges, handlebars, and a tailgate mounted spare wheel with a longer wheelbase than the previous generation.
The dual tone metal roof has a sleek black matte finish for adventure aesthetic and durability. This dual tone roof on Stealth Black vehicles is available only on AX7L and AX5L variants. The MX1, MX3, AX3L, and MX5 variants have body coloured roofs when finished in Stealth Black.
Front design features C-shaped LED Daytime Running Lamps with matching iconic LED taillamps at the rear. It has a new front grille design with polished trim. The AX7L variant includes R19 Diamond Cut Alloy Wheels set within trapezoidal wheel arches with class leading All Terrain tyres.

DIMENSIONS AND CAPACITIES
Vehicle length is 4428 mm. Width is 1870 mm. Height is 1923 mm. Wheelbase is 2850 mm which is the highest in the segment. Track width for both front and rear is 1580 mm.
Off road metrics include an approach angle of 41.7 degrees, departure angle of 36.1 degrees, and ramp over angle of 23.9 degrees. Water wading depth is 650 mm. Seating capacity is 5 people and the fuel tank capacity is 57 Liters.

PLATFORM AND CHASSIS
Built on the all new M_GLYDE platform using a high strength steel chassis. This delivers high torsional stiffness, lighter weight, and the highest wheelbase in its segment to improve stability, comfort, and handling balance.

ENGINES AND TRANSMISSIONS
The Petrol engine option is the 2.0 Liter mStallion Turbo Petrol Direct Injection TGDI with an electric wastegate turbocharger and intercooler. This engine is rear wheel drive only. The 6 speed manual petrol transmission provides maximum power of 119 kW at 5000 rpm and maximum torque of 330 Nm between 1500 and 3000 rpm. The 6 speed torque converter automatic petrol transmission provides maximum power of 130 kW at 5000 rpm and maximum torque of 380 Nm between 1750 and 3000 rpm.

The Diesel engine option is the 2.2 Liter mHawk Common Rail Direct Injection with an electric variable geometry turbocharger and intercooler. It is available in rear wheel drive and 4x4. The 6 speed manual diesel transmission provides maximum power of 111.9 kW at 3750 rpm and maximum torque of 330 Nm between 1500 and 3000 rpm. The 6 speed torque converter automatic diesel transmission with rear wheel drive provides maximum power of 111.9 kW at 3750 rpm and maximum torque of 330 Nm between 1500 and 3000 rpm. The 6 speed torque converter automatic diesel transmission with 4x4 has a higher output tune providing maximum power of 128.6 kW at 3500 rpm and maximum torque of 400 Nm between 1750 and 2750 rpm.

The 4x4 system features a manual shift part time 4x4 with high and low reduction gear, an electronic locking differential, and a 2H mode with front axle disconnect.

VARIANT LINE UP AND POWERTRAIN AVAILABILITY
The variants listed from low to high are MX1, MX3, MX5, AX3L, AX5L, and AX7L.
Petrol manual transmission is available only on MX1 and MX3. Petrol automatic transmission is available on MX5, AX5L, and AX7L. Diesel 2WD manual transmission is available across all variants including MX1, MX3, MX5, AX3L, AX5L, and AX7L. Diesel 2WD automatic transmission is available only on MX3, MX5, and AX5L. Diesel 4x4 manual and automatic transmissions are available only on the MX5 and AX7L variants.

Wheels and tyres vary by trim level. The MX1, MX3, and AX3L variants feature R18 Steel wheels with 255/65 R18 tyres. The MX5 and AX5L variants feature R18 Diamond Cut Alloy wheels with 255/65 R18 tyres. The AX7L variant features R19 Diamond Cut Alloy wheels with 255/60 R19 tyres. The spare wheel always matches the variant tyre size meaning R18 for MX1, MX3, MX5, AX3L, AX5L and R19 for AX7L.

SAFETY FEATURES
The vehicle has a 5 Star Bharat NCAP Safety Rating with an adult occupant rating of 31.09 and a child occupant rating of 45.00.
It features over 35 standard safety items. This includes up to 6 airbags covering driver, passenger, side, and curtain areas.
It includes the comprehensive ESP 9.3 suite featuring electronic stability control, EBD, ABS, corner braking control, engine drag torque control, vehicle dynamics control, hill hold control, hill descent control, hydraulic brake assist, traction control, roll over mitigation, emergency stop signal, brake locking differential, and brake disc wiping.
Additional safety systems include automatic emergency braking with vehicle pedestrian and cyclist detection depending on variant. It includes a tyre pressure monitoring system, parking sensors, rear camera, and 3 point seat belts for all seats. It features seat belt reminders with an occupant detection system, front seat belt pretensioners, height adjustable front seat belts, and a day night auto dimming interior rearview mirror. Emergency systems include impact sensing auto door unlock, engine immobilizer, E-Call, SOS, and ISOFIX or i-Size child seat compatibility. Rear disc brakes are standard on select variants. Electric park brake with auto hold is available on the Tech Pack L variants.
